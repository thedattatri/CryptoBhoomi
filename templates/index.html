Why Blockchain for Land Registry?

Tamper-Proof Records

Once recorded on the blockchain, ownership records cannot be altered or deleted by anyone, ensuring complete data integrity.

Full Transparency

All transactions are publicly verifiable on the blockchain. Anyone can verify ownership without relying on intermediaries.

Instant Transfers

Smart contracts automate ownership transfer upon payment, eliminating weeks of paperwork and manual processing.

No Middlemen

Direct peer-to-peer transfers between buyer and seller. No brokers, no agents, no unnecessary fees.

Government Verified

Revenue officers verify ownership and documents before property is listed on the blockchain, ensuring legitimacy.

Secure Payments

Ether payments through MetaMask with smart contract validation. Payment triggers automatic ownership transfer.
