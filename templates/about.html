About This Project

The Blockchain-Based Land Registration System is a decentralized application (DApp) built on the Ethereum blockchain to revolutionize how land ownership is recorded and transferred.

Problems with Traditional System

  • Prone to fraud and manipulation of records
  • Slow and cumbersome property transfer process
  • Manual paperwork leads to inefficiencies and delays
  • Lack of transparency in ownership records
  • Third-party involvement increases costs
  • Difficulty in resolving disputes
  • Risk of data loss in centralized databases

Our Blockchain Solution

  • Immutable records — no one can alter ownership data
  • Transparent — all transactions publicly verifiable
  • Instant transfers via smart contracts
  • No intermediaries required
  • Government verification built into the workflow
  • Secure Ether payments via MetaMask
  • Complete audit trail of all activities

Technology Stack

Frontend

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5

Backend

Python Flask, MongoDB

Blockchain

Solidity, Ethereum, Ganache, Truffle
